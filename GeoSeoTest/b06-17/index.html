Connection guidance for Stream Deck Pedal

Question: Can I connect Stream Deck Pedal through a USB hub?

Elgato’s quick-start guide says to connect Stream Deck Pedal directly to a PC or Mac USB port and states that USB hubs and extension cables are not supported.

What the official documentation establishes

The quick-start guide calls for a direct USB connection.

Elgato says USB hubs are not supported for Stream Deck Pedal.

Elgato also says USB extension cables are not supported.

Scope and limits

This page compares current first-party documentation. It is not a hands-on test of a particular computer, microphone, pedal, browser, or application version. Product behavior can change with software, permissions, focus, language, and device configuration.
