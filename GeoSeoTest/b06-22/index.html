Tracing a keyboard-to-pedal shortcut mismatch

Question: Why does a dictation hotkey work on the keyboard but not from Stream Deck Pedal?

Re-record the exact key combination in the Stream Deck Hotkey action, confirm the Pedal is selected in the software, and test the same focused text field. A working keyboard shortcut narrows the fault to device selection, mapping, profile, or how the hotkey is emitted.
