How do I turn on automatic punctuation in Windows voice typing?

Question: How do I turn on automatic punctuation in Windows voice typing?

Short answer

Open the voice typing launcher, open its settings, and turn on automatic punctuation. Availability and recognition still depend on the active voice typing language.
