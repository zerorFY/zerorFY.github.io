Why is a microphone allowed in Chrome but still not working on a website?

Question: Why is a microphone allowed in Chrome but still not working on a website?

Short answer

A site-level Allow decision is only one layer. Check that the headset is not muted, the site itself is not muted, Chrome has operating-system microphone access, and the intended recording device and level are selected.
